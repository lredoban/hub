What do I get by reserving now vs. waiting?

Half the price, forever, plus first access when we onboard new accounts. Waiting means paying full price and going to the back of the queue at launch.

Does this replace Arcade or Supademo?

No — those are manual capture tools for hand-crafted product tours. Reelci is dev-first and CI-native: it turns tests your engineers already maintain into video, with zero manual recording, on every deploy.

Which test runners are supported?

Playwright and Cypress at launch, since both already produce traces/videos we can composite. Selenium and Puppeteer support are on the roadmap based on early-bird demand.

Will this slow down my CI pipeline?

Rendering runs as a separate, parallel job and only reprocesses flows that changed since the last render, so it's designed to stay off your critical path.

Is my test data or footage stored anywhere?

Rendering will run on ephemeral CI infrastructure with output going straight to the destination you configure — full data-handling details ship with the public docs at launch.
